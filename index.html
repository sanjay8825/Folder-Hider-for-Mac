FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Folder Locker really free?

Yes. It is free to download and use, with no account. If it helps you, you can optionally buy me a coffee with the button at the bottom of the page.

Which version do I need, Apple Silicon or Intel?

Open the Apple menu → About This Mac. If it shows a Chip named Apple M1, M2, M3, M4 or M5, choose Apple Silicon. If it shows an Intel processor, choose Intel.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the developer?

The app is not signed with a paid Apple certificate. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

What is the first password?

The first password is 123. Open the app and use Change password straight away.

Is Quick hide encrypted?

No. Quick hide moves files out of sight instantly, whatever their size, behind an app password. Someone with technical knowledge could still find them. Use Encrypt (AES-256) for sensitive files.

What if I forget my password?

Encrypted files cannot be recovered. The app can erase everything and start fresh, which permanently deletes locked files.

What happens if I delete or update the app?

Reinstalling starts a fresh vault and removes the old one. Restore your files before deleting or updating the app.
